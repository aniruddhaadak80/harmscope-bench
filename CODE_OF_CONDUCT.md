# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

**Behaviour that builds this environment:**

- Being welcoming to newcomers, including those whose first PR is not perfect
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Assuming the other person is acting in good faith

**Behaviour that is not acceptable:**

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at aniruddhaadak80. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behaviour and are
expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
