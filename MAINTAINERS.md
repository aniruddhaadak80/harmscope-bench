## Responsibilities

- Review within five working days, or say when you will.
- Cut releases when `main` is green and `CHANGELOG.md` has a meaningful `Unreleased` section.
- Respond to security reports within 72 hours (see `SECURITY.md`).
- Keep `AGENTS.md` accurate. A stale router is worse than no router, because it sends people
  to the wrong file confidently.

## Becoming a maintainer

Consistent, high-quality review over three months. Review quality means catching real problems
— including the architectural ones — not approving style.
